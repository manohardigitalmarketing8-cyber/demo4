Most Frequently Asked Questions

Tap any question to read the answer.

Which events do you cater for?

We cater for weddings and engagements, corporate events, birthdays, anniversaries, receptions, housewarmings, traditional South Indian functions, outdoor parties and more. If you are planning a gathering, we can plan a menu for it.

How do I get a price quote?

Share your event date, guest count and preferred menu with us on WhatsApp or through the enquiry form. We will send you a detailed per-head quotation with the dishes and service included.

Do you cook on-site or in a kitchen?

It depends on the event. For most functions we prepare food at the venue, which keeps it fresh and hot. For smaller events we can cook in our kitchen and deliver ready dishes. We will recommend the best option for your venue and guest count.

Can you customize the menu?

Yes. Our menu is a starting point. We adjust dishes based on your budget, regional preferences, dietary needs and the style of your event. Just tell us what your guests enjoy.

Do you serve both veg and non-veg food?

Yes, we prepare pure veg menus as well as combined veg and non-veg menus. For events with both, we keep the sections clearly separate to avoid any mixing.

What is the minimum order size?

There is no fixed minimum for most menus, but full on-site catering works best for events of around 50 guests or more. For smaller gatherings, we offer food packages that can be delivered and set up.

How far in advance should I book?

We recommend booking at least 2-3 weeks ahead for regular events, and earlier for weddings during peak season. Last-minute bookings are welcome subject to availability.

Do you provide serving staff and setup?

Yes. Our team handles the full setup, serving and cleanup, so you can enjoy your event without worrying about the food.
